What is the Pathfinder Add-On

You can extend your LifeBots bot’s capabilities with the growing library of LifeBots specialized add-ons.

LifeBots add-ons can be purchased in the LifeBots Add-On Store at https://lifebots.cloud/store.

This article describes the LifeBots Pathfinder Add-On.

Overview

The Pathfinder add-on is an advanced navigation system for your bot with waypoint management, patrol routes, and obstacle avoidance.

The LifeBots Pathfinder add-on works with both Lite and Full LifeBots Bot plans.

Features of the Pathfinder add-on include:

  • ✅ Multiple waypoint management
  • ✅ Scheduled patrol routes
  • ✅ Basic obstacle avoidance
  • ✅ Create guide/quest bots

Purchase the LifeBots Pathfinder Add-On at https://lifebots.cloud/store/addon/pathfinder

Waypoint Navigator

The Pathfinder Add-On includes the Waypoint Navigator which can be used to create a sequence of movements and actions for your bot to follow automatically.

Use the Waypoint Navigator to automate your Bot’s movements and actions to:

  • ✅ Create sequences of locations for your bot to visit
  • ✅ Add actions like chatting, sitting, animations, and touch interactions
  • ✅ Set up patrol routes with timing options
  • ✅ Create complex automated behaviors
